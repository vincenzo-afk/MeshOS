MeshOS turns any LAN — or invited devices anywhere — into a single collaborative workspace. Chat, transfer files, share your clipboard, whiteboard, and screen, all sent directly browser-to-browser over encrypted WebRTC. Nothing ever touches a server.

Direct P2P File Transfer

Drag, drop, and send files or entire folders straight to any peer — chunked, resumable, and queued.

Real-Time Chat

Public rooms, DMs, threads, reactions, read receipts, and searchable history.

Collaborative Whiteboard

Draw, sketch, and brainstorm together with pens, shapes, and sticky notes — see everyone's live cursors, name tags, and in-progress strokes as they draw.

Shared Clipboard

Broadcast copied text, links, and snippets to everyone — or just the people you choose.

Screen Sharing

Share a window, tab, or your whole screen with permission-based viewing.

Live Network Map

Watch your workspace's topology animate in real time, with latency and quality per link.

Collaborative Terminal

A friendly, educational command line for exploring your mesh network together.

Encrypted by Design

WebRTC transport encryption plus optional AES-GCM end-to-end encryption — with zero server-side storage.

Your Desktop, Your Style

Windows, macOS, Ubuntu, Cyberpunk, Hacker, and Minimal themes, in light or dark.

MeshOS is an open-source, browser-based LAN collaboration platform. No files are ever stored on the server.
